## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Any member's finished game can be studied

A member SHALL be able to open any finished game of any member on the analysis board, read its review, and save it as
a study, from the member's profile and from the game page. A game still being played SHALL NOT be opened beside the
engine.

#### Scenario: Studying a club member's game

- **WHEN** Alice opens Bob's profile and picks Analyse on one of his finished games
- **THEN** the game opens on the analysis board, and Save as study keeps it in Alice's studies
